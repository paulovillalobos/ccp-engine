5. Build: v12 trained (1 965 steps, 2 epochs, 35 470 examples) and was registered as leitor:a14.
6. Build: **serving bug**. The test rows store the answer schema with its keys in alphabetical order
   (`nao_tipavel` before `regras`). The decoder forces the model to write keys in the schema's order, and v12 was
   trained in the factory's order. So v12 had to start every answer with «nao_tipavel», and said «not typable» on
   100 of 104 dev answers (the gold: 25). Sealed settled 10.4 % that way. The fix is `--order required` in the Modal
   reader: it puts the keys back in the factory's order, checked on all 593 measured rows. It was found from the data,
   not from the sealed answers. Attempt 1 is kept as `leitor-a14.attempt1.jsonl`. The same trap had been written down
   in phase 4, and the v12 data generator repeated it.
7. Build: v12 re-read dev, sealed and old dev with the fix; measured; round 8 recorded; verify run; committed and
   pushed.
